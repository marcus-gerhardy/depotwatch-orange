# Savings plan calculator
What a regular bitcoin savings plan would have returned, or might return.

## Overview {#dca-overview}
On the left, enter how much you buy, how often and over which period. Optionally add a one-off amount on the start day and a fee per purchase. After **Calculate**, the results appear on the right, with the chart below.

The calculator neither reads nor changes your portfolio file. Only this browser remembers your inputs.

> The results are not financial advice.

## Prices {#dca-prices}
The prices come from the crypto exchange Binance. For each day, the last price before midnight (universal time, UTC) counts.

- From early 2020: bitcoin in euros.
- From August 2017 to the end of 2019: bitcoin in US dollars, converted with the official euro rate of the European Central Bank.
- There are no prices before August 2017.

If a day has no price, the next day's price counts. Prices are only loaded when you calculate. More is behind the **Price sources** link at the top right of the calculator.

## Purchase days {#dca-schedule}
Monthly and quarterly plans always buy on the day of the month you started. If a month does not have that day, the purchase happens on its last day, and on the original day again the month after.

Example, start on 31 January: purchases on 31 Jan, 29 (or 28) Feb, 31 Mar, 30 Apr.

## Estimate for the future {#dca-projection}
For the time after the last known price, the calculator estimates. It continues how strongly the price has risen over the years so far. In the chart the estimate starts at the vertical line; it is dashed, and the range from cautious to optimistic is dotted. The results give the same range.

> Nobody knows whether the past trend continues. The estimate is no forecast.

## Results {#dca-figures}
- **Final value**: what your bitcoin are worth at the end.
- **Average purchase price**: what one bitcoin cost you on average, without fees.
- **Total return**: final value compared with everything you paid in.
- **Return per year**: converted to one year and weighted by how long each euro was invested.
